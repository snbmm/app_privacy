"Hotter Dog" Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy describes how "Hotter Dog" (the "App") handles your information.

1. No Personal Data Collection (Offline First)

"Hotter Dog" is designed to prioritize your privacy. All game logic, including the AI semantic similarity calculations, runs entirely offline on your device using local resources. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, gameplay logs, or text guesses to external servers.

2. Local Storage

The App uses local device storage (such as localStorage / web view storage) exclusively to:

  • Save your level progression (unlocked levels and stars).
  • Store your local guess history.
  • Save your offline settings (such as Background Music preferences).

This data remains on your physical device and is not synchronized with any external databases.

3. Third-Party Ads (Google AdMob)

The App integrates the Google AdMob SDK to serve rewarded ads (which allow you to unlock hints or skip levels).

  • If you grant permission via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, Google AdMob may use device identifiers (such as IDFA) to deliver personalized ads.
  • If you decline, AdMob will only serve non-personalized ads.
  • Google AdMob may collect and use data in accordance with its own privacy policy. You can read more about how Google uses data here: Google Ads Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since all gameplay processing is local, no personal data from minors is ever transmitted.
